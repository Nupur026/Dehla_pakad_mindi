How to play Dehla Pakad

Teams: Team 1 is You + Riya. Team 2 is Arjun + Karan.

Play: Each player plays one card per trick. Follow the suit led if you have it.

Cut / trump: When a player cannot follow suit for the first time, a trump suit is revealed (different from the lead suit). A trump card can cut the trick. The winning card and any cut are shown in a separate panel after each trick.

Dehle: Each captured 10 is one Dehla point. Captured 10s are highlighted in the team's capture pile.

Round: 13 tricks are played. The team with more Dehle wins. The final score is shown as Team 1 Wins X–Y or Team 2 Wins Y–X.
